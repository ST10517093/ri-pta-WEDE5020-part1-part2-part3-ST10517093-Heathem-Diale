About Halwas

Who we are

Authentic food with a welcoming spirit.

Halwas African Restaurant is an African restaurant at 13 Pretorius Street, Pretoria Central. The restaurant is positioned around authentic African and Ugandan cuisine, fresh produce, dine-in and takeaway services, and event catering.

Our menu celebrates comforting flavours and dishes that connect people to African food traditions. We aim to make quality African food approachable for residents, students, families, tourists and office workers.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, authentic African food in a welcoming environment while creating a memorable experience for every customer.

Our Vision

To become a recognised destination for African cuisine in Pretoria and a trusted choice for everyday meals and catering.

Our values

What matters to us
